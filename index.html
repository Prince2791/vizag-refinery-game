Opening scene

It is 8:15 a.m. at Vizag Coastal Refinery. Overnight crude shipments have become uncertain after conflict in a key shipping corridor. Insurance premiums are higher. Crude quality is uneven. The distillation team is under pressure to keep output moving.

The Production Manager says the refinery is still efficient. The Cost Accountant is not so sure. The CFO wants two answers: first, the weighted-average process cost report; second, whether ABC reveals hidden order-level support costs.

Your mission: complete the pre-test, solve the process-costing and ABC game, complete the post-test, and submit a short CFO recommendation.
Important: Process costing uses weighted-average only. Do not use FIFO.
